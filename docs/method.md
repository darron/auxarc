# What to change in CAD

**Use the working wing’s hinge on the magazine carrier.**

The wing already fits. The carrier’s hinge is tighter. Plastic between its
teeth overlaps the holster in the model.

## Make this change

1. Line up the wing and carrier pin holes.
2. Replace the carrier’s three hinge teeth and nearby neck with the wing’s.
3. **Copy the bottoms of the gaps too.** Narrowing the teeth alone misses the collision.
4. Join that hinge to the carrier body with **0.5 mm of overlap**.
5. Keep the magazine pocket and mounting features at their original size.

All dimensions below are in millimeters.

| Feature | Original carrier | Copied wing hinge |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tooth width | 9.8 | 9.6 |
| Outside hinge diameter, approximate | 7.9 | 7.5 |
| Tooth spacing | 20 | 20 |
| Pin-hole diameter | 2.2 | 2.2 |

## What worked

- The revised carrier seats in the printed holster.
- The filament pin starts entering the last hinge section.
- **Still tight. Full pin passage is not confirmed.**
